Welcome to your personal health companion!

This diabetes predictor app is designed to help you understand your potential risk for developing type 2 diabetes.

By analyzing key health indicators like your age, weight, family history, and lifestyle factors, our tool provides an instant, data-driven risk assessment. It's a powerful first step toward gaining awareness about your metabolic health and can be a great conversation starter with your doctor.

A Very Important Note: Please remember that this assessment is a screening tool and not a medical diagnosis. No algorithm can be 100% accurate, and many factors influence your health. The result provided by this app is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

We strongly encourage you to share your results with a healthcare professional for a proper evaluation and personalized guidance. Your health journey is important—let this app be your first step, not your last.
